chapter configures it and hands =SPC= to [[file:41-leader.org][the kittymacs leader]].

* How Meow thinks

Meow is modal, like Vim, but the order is reversed. In Vim you say *what to do*
and then *what to do it to* (=d w=, delete a word). In Meow you first *select*
something, extend the selection if you like, and then *act* on it (=w= marks
a word, =d= deletes the selection). Because the selection is visible before
you act, you always see what a command will touch, and Meow can print little
hints on screen while you extend with =e= or a digit.

Meow has a few *states*, shown in the mode line:

- *Normal*: keys select and act. This is where you live.
- *Insert*: keys type text. =i=, =a=, =c= and =A=/=I= enter it; =ESC= leaves.
- *Motion*: for read-only application buffers such as Dired, Magit and Help.
  The buffer keeps its own keys; only =j=/=k= and the leader are added.
- *Beacon*: Meow's multiple-cursor mode. Unchanged here; see =M-x meow-tutor=.

Run =M-x meow-tutor= once. It teaches the grammar interactively in an hour.

* The grammar

Meow ships no default Normal-state keys; every configuration defines its own.
This one keeps the layout Meow's own documentation and =meow-tutor= describe,
so upstream help transfers. The lower-case key moves or selects, and the
shifted key does the same while *extending* the current selection. Digits
pick one of the numbered expansion hints Meow draws after a movement.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(defun kittymacs-meow-setup ()
  "Install the QWERTY selection grammar and the Motion-state basics."
  ;; Motion state: application buffers keep their own keys; only j/k move.
  (meow-motion-define-key
   '("j" . meow-next)
   '("k" . meow-prev))
  (meow-normal-define-key
   ;; Numbered expansion hints and counts.
   '("0" . meow-expand-0)
   '("9" . meow-expand-9)
   '("8" . meow-expand-8)
   '("7" . meow-expand-7)
   '("6" . meow-expand-6)
   '("5" . meow-expand-5)
   '("4" . meow-expand-4)
   '("3" . meow-expand-3)
   '("2" . meow-expand-2)
   '("1" . meow-expand-1)
   '("-" . negative-argument)
   ;; Move; the shifted key extends the selection instead.
   '("h" . meow-left)
   '("H" . meow-left-expand)
   '("j" . meow-next)
   '("J" . meow-next-expand)
   '("k" . meow-prev)
   '("K" . meow-prev-expand)
   '("l" . meow-right)
   '("L" . meow-right-expand)
   '("b" . meow-back-word)
   '("B" . meow-back-symbol)
   '("e" . meow-next-word)
   '("E" . meow-next-symbol)
   '("g" . beginning-of-buffer)
   '("G" . end-of-buffer)
   ;; Select a thing: word, symbol, line, block, or by character.
   '("w" . meow-mark-word)
   '("W" . meow-mark-symbol)
   '("x" . meow-line)
   '("o" . meow-block)
   '("O" . meow-to-block)
   '("f" . meow-find)
   '("t" . meow-till)
   '("n" . meow-search)
   '("v" . meow-visit)
   '(";" . meow-reverse)
   '(":" . meow-goto-line)
   '("," . meow-inner-of-thing)
   '("." . meow-bounds-of-thing)

   '("]" . meow-end-of-thing)
   ;; Act on the selection.
   '("a" . meow-append)

   '("m" . meow-join)
   '("p" . meow-yank)
   '("y" . meow-clipboard-save)
   '("u" . meow-undo)

   '("z" . meow-pop-selection)
   '("q" . meow-quit)
   '("'" . repeat)
   '("&" . meow-query-replace-regexp)

   '("=" . meow-grab)

   '("Y" . meow-sync-grab)

* Things, starting states and package shims

A *thing* is what =,= and =.= select: a word, a pair of brackets, a string.
Angle brackets are added to the built-in set on =<=. =meow-mode-state-list= decides
the state a buffer starts in, and each package chapter adds its own entries
next to the package: Dired and Magit start in Motion, shells and terminals in
Insert because their keys belong to the program. Once everything is set, Meow
is enabled globally and the leader is attached.

The numbered expansion hints keep their colours from the theme: Meow mixes
the cursor colour into the region colour for the hint backgrounds and
recomputes them each time a theme is enabled (=meow-use-dynamic-face-color=,
left at its default of =t=), so the hints stand out from plain text and
follow =SPC t t=. Neither doom-themes nor the Sonokai port styles these
faces, so switching the option off, as the setup this was adapted from did,
leaves the hints indistinguishable from text.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(use-package meow
  :ensure t
  :custom
  (meow-use-cursor-position-hack t)
  (meow-use-clipboard t)
  (meow-goto-line-function #'consult-goto-line)
  :config
  (meow-thing-register 'angle '(regexp "<" ">") '(regexp "<" ">"))
  (add-to-list 'meow-char-thing-table '(?< . angle))
  (kittymacs-meow-setup)
  (meow-global-mode 1)
  (kittymacs-leader-enable))

#+end_src

* Things from the syntax tree

In a Tree-sitter mode (see [[file:74-languages.org][the languages chapter]]) the buffer has a real
parse tree, and =meow-tree-sitter= turns its nodes into Meow things: =, f=
selects inside the current function and =. f= the whole function; =a= is a
class, =t= a test, =y= an entry, =,= a parameter, =/= a comment. In modes
without a grammar these keys simply report that there is no parser.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(use-package meow-tree-sitter
  :ensure t
  :after meow
  :config
  (meow-tree-sitter-register-defaults))

#+end_src
