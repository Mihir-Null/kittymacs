- Five small packages that extend [[id:3fd783cd-deb8-48aa-bb44-0d9b16535365][the defaults]].
- They live apart because the defaults load before =private.el= and host files, too early for anything to choose a package or leave it out.
  + So this module loads right after them, and every package here can be a choice in [[id:3f8a6c02-9d14-4b7e-a2c5-1e7d90b64f31][the catalogue]].
- The undo tree and whitespace cleanup are in the =minimal= profile; =window-picker=, =popups= and =color-names= each sit behind a =when=, with their keys.
- Distilled from Lambda-Emacs by Colin McLear: its =lem-setup-windows= and =-colors= modules.
- [[id:f1d045c2-e2dc-4514-ab17-dd982ed6be00][The frames chapter]] decides where popups appear.

* Whitespace

- =ws-butler= keeps whitespace tidy: it removes trailing whitespace only on lines you changed, so a commit never touches lines you did not edit.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(use-package ws-butler
  :ensure t
  :hook ((text-mode prog-mode) . ws-butler-mode))

#+end_src

* The undo tree :meow:
:PROPERTIES:
:ID: 808d7464-14c2-42c5-865e-c08e17141ae2
:END:

- After undoing past a fork, =SPC b u= draws the history as a tree (Vundo).
- Vundo's buffer starts in Motion state, so its own keys work; it has no =SPC m= menu.

| Key        | In the tree                |
|------------+----------------------------|
| =f=, =b=   | move along it              |
| =n=, =p=   | change branch              |
| =RET=      | keep the state under point |

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(use-package vundo
  :ensure t
  :commands vundo
  :custom
  (vundo-glyph-alist vundo-unicode-symbols)
  (vundo-compact-display t)
  :config
  (with-eval-after-load 'meow
    (add-to-list 'meow-mode-state-list '(vundo-mode . motion))))

(keymap-set kittymacs-buffer-map "u" (cons "undo tree" #'vundo))

#+end_src

* Windows and popups

- =ace-window= jumps to a window by letter on =SPC w w=, and swaps two windows on =SPC w s=.
- Popper collects transient buffers under one toggle key:
  + =*Messages*=, =*Async Shell Command*=, any buffer ending in =Output*=, Help and compilation buffers;
  + grouped by directory, twenty lines high.

| Key     | Popper                              |
|---------+-------------------------------------|
| =M-`=   | show or hide the last popup         |
| =C-`=   | cycle through popups                |
| =C-M-`= | turn a buffer into a popup, or back |

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(when (kittymacs-feature-p 'window-picker)
  (use-package ace-window
    :ensure t
    :commands (ace-window ace-swap-window aw-flip-window))

  (keymap-set kittymacs-window-map "w" (cons "jump to window" #'ace-window))
  (keymap-set kittymacs-window-map "s" (cons "swap windows" #'ace-swap-window)))

(when (kittymacs-feature-p 'popups)
  (use-package popper
    :ensure t
    :demand t
    :bind (("M-`" . popper-toggle)
           ("C-`" . popper-cycle)
           ("C-M-`" . popper-toggle-type))
    :custom
    (popper-window-height 20)
    (popper-group-function #'popper-group-by-directory)
    (popper-reference-buffers
     '("\\*Messages\\*"
       "Output\\*$"
       "\\*Async Shell Command\\*"
       help-mode
       compilation-mode))
    :config
    (popper-mode 1)
    (popper-echo-mode 1)))

#+end_src

* Colour names

- =rainbow-mode= colours colour names in place; =SPC t r= turns it on in a buffer.

#+begin_src emacs-lisp
(when (kittymacs-feature-p 'color-names)
  (use-package rainbow-mode
    :ensure t
    :commands rainbow-mode)

  (keymap-set kittymacs-toggle-map "r" (cons "colour names" #'rainbow-mode)))

(provide 'kittymacs-basics)

#+end_src

* Check

- Add spaces to the end of a line you edited, wait to save: they are gone, and untouched lines keep theirs.
- Undo a few edits, make a new one, then =SPC b u=: the tree shows the fork.
- =SPC w w= with several windows in a frame: each gets a letter.
- =M-`= toggles the last popup.
- =SPC t r= in a buffer that names a colour: the name shows in that colour.
